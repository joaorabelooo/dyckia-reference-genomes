# Conserved chromosomes, divergent evolutionary paths

## Chromosome-scale genomics of rupicolous Dyckia

Supplementary materials accompanying the manuscript **Conserved chromosomes, divergent evolutionary paths: chromosome-scale genomics of rupicolous Dyckia**, by João Victor da Silva Rabelo-Araujo and coauthors, also included in the author's doctoral thesis.

The study presents chromosome-scale, haplotype-resolved reference assemblies for *Dyckia consimilis* and *D. rariflora* and compares repetitive DNA, chromosome collinearity, gene annotation, orthogroups and molecular evolution.

## Figures and scope

The current GBE manuscript designates Figures 1-6 as main-text figures and contains no supplementary-figure calls. GenomeScope profiles are Figure 1 in that version, although an older supplementary document called them Figure S1. They have not been duplicated or assigned a new supplementary number here.

This deposit contains the cited supplementary tables. Custom analysis scripts, editable manuscript drafts and reviewer correspondence are not included. Large genomic datasets remain in the archives cited in the manuscript.
